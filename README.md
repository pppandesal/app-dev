# 🦸 Invincible

**Invincible** is an American adult animated superhero series created by **Robert Kirkman**, streaming on **Amazon Prime Video**.  
It is based on the **Image Comics** series co-created with **Cory Walker** and **Ryan Ottley**.  

The show follows **Mark Grayson**, a teenager discovering his powers while dealing with his father, **Omni-Man**, a powerful alien hero with a dark secret.  
Packed with intense action, emotional storytelling, and shocking twists, **Invincible** is a must-watch for superhero fans.
